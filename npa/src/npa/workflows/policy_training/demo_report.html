Approved checkpoint

SHA256 verified against numeric checkpoint bytes before evaluation and policy test. Both holdouts remain evaluation-only. Fine-tuning adapts the training demonstrations to a simulated actuator with half the nominal gain.

How success is measured

Reach within 4 cm of a held-out target after 16 control steps. Each holdout has six independent source episodes, each tested from four initial-state variations (24 correlated trials). The two pretraining conditions use the same evaluator with different actuator gains. Gates require at least 90% success per condition.

Robot links illustrate measured Cartesian motion using inverse kinematics. This analytical environment does not model contacts, collisions, or real robot dynamics. Preview labels come from the generator; no object detector is run.
