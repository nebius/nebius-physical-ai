# AprilTag evidence media attribution

The four PNGs in this directory are review derivatives of photographs from the
NASA Swarmathon competition at Kennedy Space Center. The pinned AprilTag
upstream identifies its test media as
[CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/).

Modifications: the workload adds cyan expected-corner polygons, magenta
detector polygons and provenance text. Review sheets enlarge the exact overlay
with nearest-neighbor sampling and add four nearest-neighbor crops. The shifted
negative control deliberately translates detector polygons for judge
calibration. These derivatives are offered under
[CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/).

The detector source is separate from the media and remains under the pinned
upstream BSD-2-Clause license.
