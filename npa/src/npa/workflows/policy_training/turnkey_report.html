What each visual proves

  • Robot footage: native LIBERO/MuJoCo physics driven by actions returned over HTTP from the exact promoted checkpoint; NVIDIA GPU rendering.
  • Camera thumbnails: real workspace and wrist observations sent to the policy, using LeRobot's native coordinate convention.
  • Action bars and response timing: actual applied action vectors and measured server/network responses, synchronized to the footage.
  • Training curves: native optimizer metrics from continued multi-task training and task-specific fine-tuning.
  • Stage evidence: FiftyOne selection feeds the training episode list; both reserved demonstration sets are evaluated; each measured gate controls its own retry loop.
  • Episode selector: every deployment trial, including failures, on a third initial-state set untouched by model selection.

This is public SmolVLA continued training and simulator qualification. It does not prove in-house foundation pretraining, Slurm/Soperator execution, physical robot transfer, or concurrent-client batching. The SDG pipeline is separate.
