01 / SCENE

Captured RGB with calibration

Keep camera poses, intrinsics and scale references alongside the scene. Calibrate the reconstruction before metric robot interaction. This adapter reruns the capture through Lyra to retain depth; an existing Gaussian PLY alone lacks colliders.

02 / PHYSICS

Separate static and movable objects

Background colliders stay static. Manipulated objects need separate geometry, mass, friction, joints and task bindings. Appearance alone does not supply these properties.

03 / WRIST QUALITY

Validate the camera you will use

Compare held-out wrist views and contact regions. Shorter reconstruction chunks can help long sequences; dynamic arms, missing viewpoints and occlusions still need capture-specific inspection.
