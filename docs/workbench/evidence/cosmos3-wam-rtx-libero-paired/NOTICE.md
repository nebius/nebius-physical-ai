# Sources for the microwave recordings

The video shows new simulator renders from [LIBERO](https://github.com/Lifelong-Robot-Learning/LIBERO/tree/8f1084e3132a39270c3a13ebe37270a43ece2a01), distributed under the [MIT license](https://github.com/Lifelong-Robot-Learning/LIBERO/blob/8f1084e3132a39270c3a13ebe37270a43ece2a01/LICENSE). The policy ran in [NVIDIA Cosmos Framework](https://github.com/NVIDIA/cosmos-framework/tree/cf5d68c00d97ccd2480a2320ed652b92dec63102) using Cosmos 3 Nano and LIBERO training data. [Pinned revisions](results.json) · [NVIDIA OpenMDW license](../../../../skills/LICENSE-NVIDIA-COSMOS3-OPENMDW-1.1)

We trained the policy on LIBERO-10 and ran two new episodes using its predicted actions. We converted the evaluation GIFs into one labeled H.264 video showing the simulator camera for each checkpoint.
