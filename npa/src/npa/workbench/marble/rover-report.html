          WHAT THIS COLLECTION SUPPORTS
          
Perception and navigation experiments

          

            Use synchronized images, depth, actions, and poses to prototype
            visual odometry, mapping, obstacle perception, and
            action-conditioned models. This rollout uses a scripted feedback
            controller.
          

          

            For RL, connect observations and actions to a task with rewards,
            resets, and held-out evaluation. No RL policy was trained in this
            collection. Geometry is approximate and uses the provider’s
            estimated scale; physical-site accuracy and sim-to-real transfer
            remain unmeasured.
