The six PNGs in this directory are review derivatives of photographs from the NASA Swarmathon competition at Kennedy Space Center. The pinned AprilTag upstream identifies its test media as [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/).

Modifications: the workload adds cyan expected-corner polygons, magenta detector polygons and provenance text. Review sheets enlarge the exact overlay with nearest-neighbor sampling and add four nearest-neighbor crops. These derivatives are offered under [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/).

The detector source is separate from the media and remains under the pinned upstream BSD-2-Clause license.

For each named `_review.png`, the corresponding `_submitted.png` is a second derivative of the same credited photograph under the same CC BY-SA 2.0 terms. It is the product-generated RGB thumbnail at 674×768, extracted unchanged from the actual model request. No generative image editing was applied.
