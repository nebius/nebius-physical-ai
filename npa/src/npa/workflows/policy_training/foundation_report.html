What this demo shows

This is an interactive architecture preview of foundation-model training on Soperator. Motion depicts data flow, model structure, and scheduling concepts; it is not measured GPU telemetry. Videos are public LIBERO demonstrations, not predictions from a model trained in this run.

The intended public model is NVIDIA GR00T N1.7 3B. Its required backbone access is pending, so this artifact contains no foundation-training loss, benchmark success rate, or claim of a completed GPU run. Fine-tuning a pretrained model is distinct from training a foundation model from scratch.

Data generation is outside this training pipeline. Source footage is from lerobot/libero, licensed Apache-2.0. Clips are extracted from existing dataset videos and transcoded to H.264; source episode, timestamps, immutable revision and checksums are recorded in evidence.json.
