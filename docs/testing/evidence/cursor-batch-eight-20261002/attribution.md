# Media attribution

`apriltag-detections.png` is an unmodified copy of the fresh workload's annotated output, derived from a NASA/Kim Shiflett photograph. [Original photograph](https://www.flickr.com/photos/nasakennedy/33369213973/). The [pinned AprilTag media notice](https://github.com/AprilRobotics/apriltag/blob/94be783968e5091bcc9972c72c84fd63efce2935/test/data/README.md) identifies this test media as [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/); this annotated derivative is offered under the same license. The workload adds cyan expected corners, magenta detected corners and provenance text. The detector source license is separate (BSD-2-Clause).

`evo-controls.png` shows synthetic trajectory controls generated by the workload and plotted by evo. These are synthetic test paths, not customer trajectories.

The Open3D benchmark recording, screenshots and geometry projections derive from `DemoICPPointClouds` / augmented ICL-NUIM. Credit: Choi et al. (2015), and A. Handa, T. Whelan, J. B. McDonald and A. J. Davison (2014), *A Benchmark for RGB-D Visual Odometry, 3D Reconstruction and SLAM*. The [Open3D dataset notice](https://www.open3d.org/docs/latest/cpp_api/classopen3d_1_1data_1_1_demo_i_c_p_point_clouds.html) and [ICL-NUIM dataset page](https://www.doc.ic.ac.uk/~ahanda/VaFRIC/iclnuim.html) identify the data as [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). The derivatives retain that attribution. Changes include registration, pose-graph fusion, Poisson surface reconstruction, support-distance filtering, visualization and diagnostic coloring. This is synthetic benchmark data; no real sensor-capture or customer-data claim applies.

`open3d-native-viewer.png` instead shows the workload's separate synthetic sphere, box and cylinder control fixture in the actual Rerun viewer.
