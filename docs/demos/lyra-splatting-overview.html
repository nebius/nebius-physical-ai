02 / Choose the reconstruction path

Required inputs

    Outputs and review

    Projection is tested

    Native checks cover direction, field of view, seam wrapping, image/video order, metadata omission and synthetic 8192 × 4096 input.

    Quality needs evidence

    Inspect haze, edges, holes and view consistency. Compare matched captures and render viewpoints; more pixels alone do not establish improvement.

    Appearance has limits

    Gaussian appearance and estimated poses do not establish metric scale, collision behavior or robot contact accuracy.
