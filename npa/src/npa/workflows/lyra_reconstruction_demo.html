Bring your own capture

One reconstruction workflow.

Prepare a sharp video with overlapping views, then run the Lyra reconstruction workflow. It retains the Gaussian PLY, camera poses, predicted depth and a native rendered video. This standalone viewer requires no Isaac runtime, Cosmos service or network connection.

Reproduce with Workbench 
npa/.venv/bin/python -m npa.workflows.lyra_capture \
  --input-path ./capture.mp4 --output-path ./lyra-input

# Publish the prepared bundle to your project's private storage.
npa workbench workflow submit workflows/testing/lyra-reconstruction.yaml \
  --project <project-alias> --infra k8s/<gpu-context> --stage-src \
  --var bucket=<bucket> --var input_uri=s3://<bucket>/<input-prefix>/ \
  --var accelerator=B200:1 \
  --secret-env AWS_ACCESS_KEY_ID --secret-env AWS_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY

# After downloading the completed reconstruction bundle:
npa/.venv/bin/python -m npa.workflows.lyra_reconstruction_demo \
  --input-path ./reconstruction --output-path ./lyra-standalone.html
